What the lines mean

Township drainage channels include natural and constructed waterways. Its downloadable channel dataset explicitly labels classifications unofficial; those labels are reference information, not verified fish sightings. City classifications come from a separate municipal layer and are kept distinct.

Coverage and limits

Mapping can be incomplete or out of date. A missing line does not mean a watercourse is absent. This is a public reference map, not a survey, drainage model, property setback assessment or access guide. The City layer supplies watercourse classifications; culverts shown here come from the Township dataset only.

Sources, privacy and downloads

Contains information from Township of Langley and Langley City public mapping services, subject to their source terms. Downloads retain source IDs. Data is retrieved periodically, not live.

Searches happen in your browser. OpenStreetMap receives tile requests for the viewed area. No analytics, accounts or saved location history. Sharing puts a record ID in the URL.
